Who Owns This Site

The KEAM 2026 Rank Predictor is owned and published by GoFinanciallyFit.com, a US-based, Arizona-headquartered independent financial blog and guidance platform. GoFinanciallyFit primarily helps individuals build better money habits through practical tips, resources, and insights — and this predictor is one of a small number of tools we publish outside that core focus, built because better educational planning and better financial planning are closely related: the choices students make at this stage shape years of tuition costs, loans, and career outcomes.

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Kerala, or the Government of Kerala. This is an independent, unofficial tool. We say this plainly and repeat it throughout the site because we think it matters: students should always treat their official CEE Kerala scorecard and the published rank list as the final word, and use this predictor only as a planning aid in the meantime.

Why We Built This Predictor

KEAM's 2026 admission cycle introduced a genuinely more complex normalization formula — a new 5:3:2 weighting for Plus Two marks, and cross-board standardization that affects CBSE, ICSE/ISC, NIOS, and Kerala State Board students differently. We noticed that most existing rank predictors online still used outdated, oversimplified formulas, or assumed every student came from a single board with a single fixed marking scale.

We built this tool to reflect the actual, current methodology as accurately as we could determine from CEE Kerala's published prospectus and notifications — board-by-board, with the real 50:30:20 (Maths:Physics:Chemistry) weighting applied correctly, rather than a generic average. We also added a companion page with last year's college-wise closing ranks, so a number alone doesn't leave a student wondering what it actually means for their admission chances.

Our Approach to Accuracy

Every formula, date, and figure on this site is sourced from CEE Kerala's official prospectus, notifications, and scorecards, or from recently reported, verifiable news coverage of the 2026 KEAM cycle. Where official data isn't yet available — such as 2026 college-wise closing ranks, which are only published after each counselling round — we say so explicitly and use the most recent completed cycle (2025) as a clearly labeled reference point, rather than presenting a guess as fact.

We link directly to official government sources throughout the site — including CEE Kerala, DHSE Kerala, CBSE, CISCE, and NIOS — so students and parents can verify anything here against the primary source. If you spot something that looks outdated or incorrect, we genuinely want to know; see our Contact page for how to reach us.
