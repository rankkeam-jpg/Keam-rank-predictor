1
Overview

KEAM 2026 PRO is a standalone, client-side web application that helps Kerala engineering aspirants estimate their KEAM rank and predict college allotment probabilities. This policy explains precisely how we handle personal data.

📋

Summary: We collect your name and phone number solely to improve your user experience. All rank calculations run exclusively inside your browser and are never transmitted to our servers.

2
Data We Collect

We collect only the following personal information, voluntarily provided by you:

  • Name — to personalise your experience within the tool
  • Phone number — to enable follow-up support and improve user experience
⚠

This information is collected and stored securely. It is used exclusively for product improvement and user experience purposes, and is never sold or shared with third parties for commercial use.

The following is never collected, logged, or transmitted:

  • Entrance exam scores — attempt counts or correct answer counts you enter
  • Plus Two (DHSE) marks for Physics, Chemistry, or Mathematics
  • Your reservation category selection
  • Calculated index scores, rank estimates, or college probabilities
  • IP addresses, device fingerprints, or browser identifiers
  • Location data, first-party or third-party cookies, or tracking pixels
2b
How We Use Your Data

The name and phone number you provide are used solely for the following purposes:

  • User experience improvement — understanding how students interact with the tool helps us refine features and usability
  • Product support — we may reach out via phone to gather feedback or provide assistance
✓

Your personal data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, third-party marketers, or any external organisation.

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at rankkeam@gmail.com.

3
How the Tool Processes Data

All computation is performed in-session using browser-native JavaScript. When you click "Generate Report", your inputs are passed to a deterministic calculation engine running entirely within your browser's JavaScript runtime.

Input → Browser JavaScript Engine → Output
              ↑ stays here, never leaves

No network request is made during or after a calculation. No data is written to localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, cookies, or any other browser storage mechanism. When you close or refresh the tab, all entered values and results are permanently cleared.

4
Data Storage & Security
✓

Rank calculations: zero storage. All score inputs, index calculations, and rank predictions run entirely in your browser and are never transmitted or stored.

Name and phone number data you submit are stored securely on our backend infrastructure. We apply standard security practices including encrypted transmission over HTTPS and access controls to protect your information.

The college cutoff data used for rank predictions is hard-coded into the JavaScript file — publicly visible in the page source — representing historical trend estimates, not individual user data.

5
HTTPS & Transport Security

All pages are served exclusively over HTTPS with TLS 1.2 / 1.3 encryption. Even loading the page — the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files — is encrypted in transit between our hosting provider and your browser.

  • All connections are encrypted via SSL/TLS certificates
  • HTTP requests are automatically redirected to HTTPS
  • HSTS headers are applied where the host supports it
  • No mixed content on any page
⚠

If you access this Tool from a saved local file (file://) rather than a hosted HTTPS domain, HTTPS encryption does not apply to the page load — though in-browser computation remains entirely local regardless.

6
Third-Party Services

KEAM 2026 PRO loads one third-party resource:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) — The Inter and Instrument Serif typefaces are loaded from Google's CDN. This results in a standard font request to Google's servers on first page load. Google's own privacy policy governs what data, if any, they collect from this request. You can self-host the fonts and remove the Google Fonts link tag if preferred.

No analytics services, advertising networks, social media trackers, or any other third-party scripts are loaded or executed.

7
Accuracy Disclaimer

KEAM 2026 PRO provides estimates based on publicly available 2025–2026 trend data, the official 5:3:2 weighted PCM formula, and DHSE standardization constants. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to CEE Kerala (Commissioner for Entrance Examinations), the Government of Kerala, or any educational institution.

Results are probabilistic estimates intended to aid personal planning — they should not be treated as guaranteed predictions. Actual ranks and allotments depend on the overall applicant pool, normalization adjustments, and institutional seat matrices which change each year.
