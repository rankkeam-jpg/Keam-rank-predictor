We do not collect any personal data

1. No Personal Data Collection

KEAM Rank Predictor does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information from its users. We do not operate user accounts, contact forms, email subscriptions, or any other mechanism that would gather your name, email address, phone number, or academic scores.

Any information you type into the rank predictor (normalized score, board marks, category) is used only within your browser to compute a result. It is never sent to our servers.

2. How the Rank Predictor Works

The rank predictor is a fully client-side tool. All computation — index score calculation, rank range estimation, college matching — runs in JavaScript inside your own browser tab. When you click "Calculate my rank", no network request is made and no data leaves your device.

Closing the browser tab or navigating away permanently discards any values you entered.

3. Google Advertising & Cookies

This website displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. We do not control these cookies and have no access to the data they collect.

What Google may collect via ad cookies

  • Your IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages visited and content interacted with on this and other sites
  • Interest categories inferred from your broader browsing activity
  • Demographic information (if associated with a signed-in Google account)

Your opt-out options

  • Adjust personalised ad preferences at Google Ads Settings
  • Opt out of third-party advertising cookies at aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative
  • Block cookies entirely via your browser settings — the rank predictor will still work fully

Opting out stops personalised ads; you will still see ads but they will not be tailored to your interests. For full details on Google's data practices, see policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Our commitments regarding advertising

  • We do not pass any user-entered data to Google's advertising systems
  • We do not use Google ad data to identify individual visitors
  • We do not serve interest-based ads to users identified as under 16

4. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site in aggregate — for example, which pages are most visited, how long sessions last, and where traffic comes from. Google Analytics collects anonymised usage data (IP addresses are truncated before storage) and does not allow us to identify individual users.

You can opt out of Google Analytics across all sites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Third-Party Links

The Service contains links to external websites (official CEE Kerala portals, college sites, government resources) not operated by us. Clicking those links directs you to the third party's site under their own privacy policy. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for their content or data practices.

6. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal data, there is no risk of inadvertently collecting data from children. The rank predictor is designed for KEAM 2026 aspirants (typically 17–18 years old) and their families. No personal information from any user, regardless of age, is stored by us.

Google AdSense's age-based ad restrictions still apply — see Section 3 for details.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Because we hold no contact information for users, we cannot notify you directly — please bookmark this page and check back periodically if this matters to you.
